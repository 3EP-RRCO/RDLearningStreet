# Lesson Outline

1. **Meet USDA Rural Development**
   - Agency mission and program areas
   - Local office role
   - Packager role and boundaries

2. **Choose the Correct Program**
   - Section 502 Direct
   - Section 502 Guaranteed
   - Section 504 repair assistance

3. **Qualify the Borrower**
   - Income limits and household facts
   - Credit and repayment considerations
   - Documentation and eligibility are not the same as approval

4. **Follow the Money**
   - Annual income
   - Adjusted annual income
   - Repayment income
   - Payment assistance

5. **Inspect the Home and Location**
   - Official property eligibility map
   - Property and dwelling requirements
   - Location eligibility does not guarantee approval

6. **Package the Application**
   - Current forms and checklists
   - Documentation and discrepancy resolution
   - Professional boundaries
   - Official training and certification path

## Study method
For each lesson:
1. Read the concept.
2. Explain it aloud in your own words.
3. Work through the scenario.
4. Answer the quiz.
5. Check the current official source for any exact eligibility rule or calculation.
6. Record the source title and the date checked.
